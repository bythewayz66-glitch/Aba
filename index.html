FIELD MANUAL

How to play

Move & fight

  • WASD move · drag right mouse to orbit
  • Shift sprint · Space jump
  • Left click basic attack
  • Q R F hero skills
  • G pet skill · H tonic

Explore & live

  • E talk, shop, farm, fish
  • V mount or dismount
  • Tab switch hero (solo/host)
  • I inventory · K skills
  • C craft · P pets · M map

Co-op

  • Host opens a room code.
  • Guests join from another tab.
  • Up to three heroes, one each.
  • Host tab simulates the world.
  • T party chat
